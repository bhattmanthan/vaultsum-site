Split expenses with friends, without the awkward maths

Trips, flat rent, groceries, a dinner where one person didn't drink. Vaultsum Groups keeps track of who paid what and who owes whom, and works out the fewest payments to settle everything.

Split it the way it actually happened

  • Equal: everyone pays the same share.
  • Exact amounts: the friend who only had a coffee pays for a coffee.
  • Percentage: the bigger room pays a bigger share of the rent.

Anyone in the group can add an expense, and everyone sees the same running balance.

Fewer payments to settle up

Instead of everyone paying everyone back, Vaultsum simplifies the balances so the group settles with as few payments as possible.

Your money never passes through the app

When you settle up, Vaultsum copies your friend's UPI ID and opens your own UPI app. You make the payment there, the way you normally would, and Vaultsum records that it happened. It doesn't hold money, lend money or move money.

What goes online

Groups is the one part of Vaultsum that needs an internet connection and a sign-in (Google or phone number), because your friends need to see shared expenses. It stores only what you add to a group. Your personal accounts and transactions stay in the encrypted database on your phone. You can delete your Groups account from the app at any time. More on privacy.

Questions

Do my friends need the app?

Yes. Everyone in a group uses Vaultsum on Android and signs in with Google, so each person sees the same expenses and balances.

Does Vaultsum send or hold money?

No. Settle up copies your friend's UPI ID and opens your own UPI app, where you make the payment. Vaultsum only records that the settlement happened.

Can I split unevenly?

Yes. Each expense can be split equally, by exact amounts, or by percentage.

Does using Groups upload my personal expenses?

No. Groups only stores what you add to a group. Your personal accounts and transactions stay on your phone.

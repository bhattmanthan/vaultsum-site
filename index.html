Vaultsum

An expense tracker that stays on your phone.

Your accounts and transactions live in an encrypted database on your Android phone. No sign-up, no cloud copy, and it reads your GPay, PhonePe and bank SMS alerts so you don't have to type every payment.

Join the beta Android 8+. Closed beta on Google Play: email us and we'll send an invite to your Gmail.

What it does

Private by default

SQLCipher-encrypted database, app lock, hide-amounts mode, encrypted backups. No account needed.

Less typing

Turns GPay, PhonePe, Paytm and SBI/HDFC/ICICI and other bank alerts into transactions you confirm. No SMS permission.

Budgets and insights

Monthly budgets that warn at 80%, category breakdowns, trends and a spending calendar.

Bills on autopilot

Recurring and future-dated entries post themselves when due, with reminders.

Many currencies

Accounts in any currency, cross-currency transfers, net worth in your base currency.

Split with friends

Optional Groups: share expenses, simplify debts, settle up through your own UPI app.

What goes online

Your personal finances never leave your phone. Two things do use the internet: a once-a-day exchange-rate download (only your base currency code is sent), and Groups, which is optional, needs a Google sign-in and stores shared expenses in Firebase so your friends can see them. Details in the privacy policy.

An expense tracker that logs payments for you

Most people stop tracking expenses because typing every payment gets old in a week. Vaultsum reads the payment alerts you already get from your bank and payment apps, and turns each one into a transaction you confirm with a tap.

Get it on Google Play

How it works

  1. You pay with Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm, or your card or account is debited and your bank sends an SMS.
  2. Vaultsum spots the notification and picks out the amount, whether money went out or came in, and the merchant or person.
  3. The payment waits in a review list with the account and category filled in.
  4. You confirm it, change it, or dismiss it. Nothing is saved until you do.

Where it reads from

  • Payment apps: Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm.
  • Bank SMS alerts: SBI, HDFC and ICICI have dedicated parsers. Alerts from Axis, Kotak and most other banks, including card spends, go through a general parser that looks for an amount and a debit or credit.
  • Anything else: add it yourself in a few taps, or set up recurring entries for rent, bills and subscriptions.

Each source has its own switch, and every source is off until you turn it on. Notifications from any other app are ignored before their content is read. OTPs and promotional messages are skipped.

No SMS permission

Vaultsum never asks to read your SMS inbox. When you turn on bank alerts, it reads the notification your SMS app shows for a new message, the same text you see on your lock screen, and only keeps the ones that look like a payment.

Stays on your phone

Parsing happens on the device. Notification content is never uploaded or logged, and your transactions are stored in an encrypted database on your phone. More on privacy.

Questions

Does Vaultsum need SMS permission?

No. It never asks for READ_SMS or RECEIVE_SMS. It reads the notification your SMS app shows when a bank alert arrives, and only for the sources you switch on.

Which apps and banks does it read?

Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm notifications, and bank SMS alerts. SBI, HDFC and ICICI have dedicated parsers; alerts from other banks, including card spends, go through a general parser that recognises amounts, debits and credits.

Is anything saved without me checking it?

No. Every detected payment waits in a review list until you confirm or dismiss it.

Is my notification content uploaded?

No. Parsing runs on your phone, and the personal tracker has no server to send it to.

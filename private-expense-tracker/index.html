VaultsumExpense Tracker for Android

A private expense tracker that keeps your data on your phone

Your spending history says a lot about you. Vaultsum keeps it in an encrypted database on your Android phone, with no account to sign up for and no cloud copy.

Get it on Google Play

Encrypted on the device

Every account, transaction and budget is stored in a SQLCipher-encrypted database. The key is a random value kept in Android's encrypted storage, so the database file is unreadable on its own.

No account, no ads

You can install Vaultsum and start tracking straight away. There's no sign-up, no email, and no ads in the app.

Locked when you want it to be

  • App lock with fingerprint, face unlock or your screen PIN.
  • Hide amounts masks figures in the app, in widgets and in notifications.
  • Screen privacy: with app lock on, Vaultsum's screens are hidden from screenshots and the recent-apps view.

Exactly what goes online

  • Exchange rates: one download a day. Only your base currency code is sent.
  • Groups (optional): needs a sign-in (Google or phone number) and stores only the expenses you share with a group. How Groups works.

Nothing else leaves your phone. Payment notifications are read on the device and never uploaded. How detection works.

Backups you control

Export a password-protected, encrypted backup whenever you like, or schedule automatic daily or weekly backups to a folder you choose, such as one synced by Google Drive. Restore everything on a new phone, or merge a backup into your current data.

Questions

Do I need an account to use Vaultsum?

No. The expense tracker works without any sign-up. Only the optional Groups feature asks you to sign in, with Google or a phone number.

Where is my data stored?

In a SQLCipher-encrypted database on your phone. The encryption key is random and kept in Android's encrypted storage.

Does Vaultsum go online at all?

Twice: a once-a-day exchange-rate download that sends only your base currency code, and Groups, if you choose to use it.

How do I keep a backup?

Export a password-protected backup, or schedule automatic daily or weekly backups to a folder you pick, such as one synced by Google Drive.
